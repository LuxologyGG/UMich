    Strain as the back carbon turns

    Shape only, approximate. The course asks you to rank conformers, not to calculate energies.

    Guided run, about 5 minutes

    1. Press Side view. Find C2 (black) and C3 (blue). The bond between them is the one you look down.
    2. Press Newman view. You are now looking straight down C2 to C3. C2's three bonds meet at the center, C3 is hidden behind it and its bonds poke out from behind. Compare the 3D model with the paper drawing.
    3. Press Rotate back carbon 120° clockwise three times. The front stays still. The blue CH3 walks 6 → 10 → 2 → 6 o'clock. Watch the pills: anti, gauche, gauche.
    4. Press 60° once. Every back bond now hides behind a front bond. That is eclipsed, and the strain plot jumps.
    5. Switch to 2-Methylbutane, reset, and step through the three staggered conformers again. Count the red gauche pairs in each and rank them. The one with the most big-group gauche pairs is the least stable.
    6. Drag the model sideways while in Newman view to see the same shape from an angle, then press Newman view again to snap back.
